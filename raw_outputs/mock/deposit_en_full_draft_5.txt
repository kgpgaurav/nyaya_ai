Rules that may be relevant (confirm with a lawyer before relying on them):
- Section 73, Indian Contract Act, 1872: A rent agreement or an employment agreement is a contract. When one side breaks it, the other side can claim compensation for loss that naturally arose from the breach. Remote or indirect losses are not covered. (Recovering money through a civil court has its own time limits and procedure. Ask a lawyer or the District Legal Services Authority early.)
- Section 11, Model Tenancy Act, 2021: The Model Tenancy Act is a model law. It applies only in states that have passed a tenancy law based on it. Where it applies, a residential deposit cannot exceed two months' rent (six months for non-residential), and the landlord must refund it when taking back vacant possession, after deducting only the tenant's actual liabilities. (Rent law is state-specific. Whether this applies depends on your state and whether you have a lease or a leave-and-licence agreement.)
